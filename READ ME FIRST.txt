STEM MIXER
==========

Split any song into Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitar, Keys and Other, then turn each
part up or down, mute it, solo it, loop sections and slow it down (without
changing the pitch) so you can learn to play and sing it.


HOW TO START
------------
1. Double-click "Start Stem Mixer".

   The first time, Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC".
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". (This appears for any program you
   download that isn't from the Microsoft Store.)

2. The first time only, it sets itself up. This takes about 5-15 minutes and
   needs an internet connection. Just leave the black window open until your
   web browser opens.

3. After that it starts in a few seconds. You can also use the "Stem Mixer"
   shortcut it puts on your desktop.

Keep the black window open while you practice. Close it to quit.
(The black window will say "Stem Mixer is running".)


USING IT
--------
- Drag a song (MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, ...) onto the page, or click "Open song...".
- The first time you open a song, it is separated into parts. This takes about as
  long as the song itself (much faster with an NVIDIA graphics card). After that
  it is saved and opens instantly from the Library list.
- Each part has a volume slider, M (mute) and S (solo).
- Speed slider: 50% to 150%, and the pitch stays the same.
- Drag across any waveform to loop that section.
- Keys: Space = play/pause, Left/Right = jump 5 s, 1-6 = mute a part,
  Shift+1-6 = solo, [ and ] = slower/faster.


WHERE THINGS ARE STORED
-----------------------
- Your separated songs:  Music\Stem Mixer   (delete a folder there to remove a song)
- The program's engine:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\StemMixer   (about 1-3 GB)

To uninstall: delete this folder, the desktop shortcut, and the two folders above.
Nothing else is installed on your computer, and no administrator rights are needed.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- If setup stops with an error, check your internet connection and double-click
  "Start Stem Mixer" again. It continues where it left off.
- If it still fails, delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\StemMixer and try once more.
  (Paste %LOCALAPPDATA% into the File Explorer address bar to find it.)
- Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
